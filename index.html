Good to Know

Studio Policies

This is a home-based business. These policies help keep every appointment peaceful, safe, and professional for everyone.

🚫
No Extra Guests

Please do not bring friends, partners, or anyone else unless absolutely necessary. If you must bring someone:

  • Please inform me in advance
  • Let me know the age of the person
  • They must wait in the lobby

👶
No Children During Appointments

Children are not allowed during appointments unless they are the client being serviced. If absolutely necessary, please inform me in advance and they must remain supervised in the waiting area.

📅
Book in Advance

Secure your spot ahead of time — walk-ins are not guaranteed. Appointments are first come, first served. Early morning slots fill quickly on weekdays.

🚗
Mobile Service Info

For at-home appointments, a travel fee applies depending on your location in Winnipeg. Please confirm your address when booking. I bring all tools and supplies needed.
